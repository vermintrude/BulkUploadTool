10 November 2026

and replace ONLY ComicRox-Series-Run.js in your existing tool folder.

cover images. It is not required for your own selected/assigned photos.

re-upload is needed just to remove the old confirmation requirement.

Own photos still need to load, meet the image-size check and have their public
Shopify image links before export. Price, condition, title, quantity and chosen
duplicate-check rules remain in place. Pending files remain protected.

Record the outcome of the previously imported one-product FCBD file separately.
