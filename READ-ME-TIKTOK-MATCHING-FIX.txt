WHAT CHANGED
- Exact-title Shopify candidates take precedence over weaker shared-word suggestions. A different Harley Quinn issue no longer appears alongside exact Scientist-title matches.
- Suggestions with explicitly conflicting issue numbers, grading company/CGC grades, publication years or cover letters are excluded.
- When several exact-title Shopify records are ALL unavailable (archived, unpublished or zero stock), their TikTok listing goes directly to the remove/zero-stock checklist. This groups the same safe action; it does not merge Shopify records or select a stocked record. All handles are included in the checklist.
- If records disagree about availability, manual review remains necessary.
- When a saved/manual match resolves an exact-title duplicate, the other exact-title records are blocked from new additions too. They cannot be recreated simply because another duplicate was selected.
- The earlier dropdown stability fix and comparison details are included.
