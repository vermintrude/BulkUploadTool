ComicRox 2.6.2 — Lunar held products and retry

YOUR EDITS ARE RETAINED
The full saved batch remains intact. Titles, selling prices and edited
ComicRox descriptions are not removed when incomplete products are held.
Download a Lunar backup from Saved Work & History before installing.

INSTALL
Stop the current server. Keep your old Live folder as Live-before-2.6.2.
Extract the complete ZIP and copy all app files into a new Live folder.
Keep Shopify-env-dont-delete and your existing external settings in place.
Start START-COMICROX-TOOL.bat. Keep the SAME browser/profile and localhost:18789
origin. Ctrl+F5 refresh; header should show V2.6.2 LUNAR HELD PRODUCTS & RETRY.
If present, copy ComicRox-API-Usage.ndjson from old Live for cost history.
If absent, skip it. Never clear browser/site data or Lunar working data.
Rollback: stop the new server and restore the old Live folder. New held-product
metadata is additive and remains in the existing Lunar backup/history key.
Keep the new backup first; folder rollback does not undo browser changes.

EXPORT THE READY BOOKS
1. Open your existing prepared week through Saved Work & History.
2. Check Shopify & Export: load a fresh Shopify ALL-products CSV and tick its
   confirmation. Either order now works; you need not choose the file again.
3. The page lists ready/existing/held counts and the exact reasons for holds.
4. Download Lunar Shopify CSV still requires every new product to be ready.
   To continue with only valid products, choose the NEW button:
   DOWNLOAD READY ONLY — KEEP HELD FOR LATER.
5. Confirm the counts. The tool checks public image links before export;
   missing URLs and images that cannot load are held with a specific reason.
   Internet problems/timeouts can also cause a hold; this does not prove a
   supplier image has been permanently removed. Retry once accessible.
6. Only ready new products go into the CSV. All records remain saved. An entry
   is recorded as pending before download; the status names the exact file
   and tells you to find it using browser Downloads (Ctrl+J).
7. Import the generated CSV ONCE into Shopify and record its import outcome.
   Existing products and pending CSVs retain their duplicate protections.

RETRY THE HELD PRODUCTS LATER
- A reminder appears when a Lunar batch is opened if anything is waiting.
- Saved Work & History has a Held for later list, grouped by original batch.
- OPEN & RETRY HELD PRODUCTS reopens that original saved batch and retries
  official Lunar product data. Manual titles, prices and descriptions remain
  authoritative. Missing image URLs may be filled when Lunar supplies them.
- This is a user-started retry, not an unattended background job. A supplier
  image cannot be invented if none is available; an exact approved image URL
  can instead be entered manually.
- After retry, use a fresh Shopify ALL-products CSV. Already-imported products
  are excluded. Recovered valid products are automatically eligible again;
  there is no permanent exclude flag to remember to undo.
- Queue entries move out of Held when exported, while the generated file stays
  in pending import history until its outcome is recorded.
- Do not choose Delete saved batch: retry opens the source with its live edits.
  Held snapshots are retained for recovery if a source batch was deleted.

OTHER FEEDBACK FIXES
The misleading LOCKED label is replaced by OPEN SAVED BATCH, LOAD SHOPIFY
EXPORT or SHOPIFY CHECKED. Checking a file with no active batch points you to
Saved Work & History. Exact hold reasons replace the previous combined generic
list. No-file-created and successful-download messages are explicit.
