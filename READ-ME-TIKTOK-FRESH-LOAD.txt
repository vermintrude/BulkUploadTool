COMICROX — FRESH TIKTOK LOAD — 3 NOVEMBER 2026

INSTALL
Keep a spare copy of ComicRox-TikTok.js in your current tool folder.
Replace ONLY that file with the supplied ComicRox-TikTok.js.
Open TikTok Sync in your normal browser and press Ctrl+F5.
Do not clear browser data. No Shopify, Cloudflare or server changes are required.

YOUR FRESH LOAD
1. Verify TikTok Live, Draft, Failed and Deactivated are ALL empty.
2. In the new "Starting again with an empty TikTok catalogue?" section:
   DOWNLOAD TIKTOK BACKUP, tick the empty-catalogue confirmation, then
   START / CONTINUE FRESH LOAD.
3. Extract products_export_1.csv from your Shopify ZIP. Load that CSV into
   Shopify master CSV. No TikTok catalogue export is needed for this fresh load.
4. Load the supplied current TikTok XLSX under "Optional current create template".
   This must be the Upload in bulk CREATE template, not a Bulk edit export.
5. Check your parcel profiles and confirm packing. Singles default to the
   previously supplied 145g / 33 x 21 x 0.5cm; confirm those suit your packing.
   Graded books and bundles require suitable packed measurements.
6. REVIEW FRESH CATALOGUE LOAD. Check Ready additions and Held.
   Fresh loads always exclude preorders and future release dates, even if an
   older available-stock override was saved. They can be added after release.
   Zero/negative stock, draft and unpublished Shopify products cannot be added.
7. Download additions XLSX. Upload that file in TikTok Seller Center:
   Products -> Bulk actions -> Upload in bulk (CREATE, not bulk EDIT).
   Review TikTok validation and publication results there.
8. Record the successful seller_sku values against that import run in the tool.
   Use the workbook's seller_sku values, not TikTok's numeric product IDs.
   Pending rows remain blocked. Release only confirmed failed/not-uploaded rows.

OLD HISTORY IS RETAINED
Starting a fresh catalogue separates old upload history from the new load.
It does not mark old successes as failures or delete saved upload files, SKU
mappings, packing settings, identity decisions, or any other tool's data.
Pending/successful rows in the NEW catalogue stay protected against duplicates.
After refreshing the page with nothing uploaded yet, continue the same fresh
load; do not start another catalogue just to retry.

AFTER THIS FIRST LOAD
Use normal Full catalogue comparison with current Shopify and TikTok exports
for ongoing additions, stock checks and cleanup. Do not confirm an empty
catalogue when products now exist. Add newly released/received books after
Shopify has updated; never include stock merely because a date has arrived.
Selected products mode also retains duplicate protection for the new catalogue.

Only if you delete another entire catalogue in future, use the separate
"Deleted a later catalogue as well?" control after downloading a new backup
and confirming that all statuses are empty again.

This creates local upload files. It does not upload to TikTok, publish products,
synchronise sales, or change Shopify quantities automatically.
